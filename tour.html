The Hartley party

A made-up charter, Saturday 17 to Saturday 24 July 2027, Antibes to Monaco, to show how SABAI collects guest preferences before a charter. Every name and detail is invented. The page is in test mode: anything entered here only emails mate@my-sabai.com.

1What the main contact receives

Before the charter, the main contact (the guest who organises the party, or the broker) receives this email with the welcome PDF. The last page of the PDF has the QR code and the Open the page button.

To: the main contact · Attached: SABAI Guest Preferences - Welcome.pdf

Dear Charlotte,

Thank you for choosing SABAI. The Captain and crew are looking forward to welcoming the Hartley party on board in Antibes on Saturday 17 July.

To help us prepare, please tell us a little about your party and each guest on our guest preferences page. The link and a QR code are on the last page of the attached PDF. You can fill in each guest yourself or send them their own link. Only names, adult or under 18, and allergies are needed; everything else is optional.

We would be grateful for the answers by Friday 25 June.

With best wishes,
The Captain and crew of SABAI

2Fill it in as the main contact

The QR code and the button in the PDF open this page. The party details and four of the six guests are already in, as they might be a week before the reply-by date.

  • Fill in Jack Hartley (under 18), as a parent would. It takes about ten minutes; only his name, age group and allergies are needed.
  • Or add a guest and press Send a link, to see the personal link a guest gets on their own phone.
  • Open Charlotte, Tom or Olivia and press Change to see their answers. James answered on his own link, so the main contact sees only that he has sent them.

3What the crew see

Every crew email links to the charter's crew page. The Overview is a one-page charter sheet: first arrival, last departure, guests, allergies, travel, meal times and the main contact. The other tabs hold the galley, interior, deck and Captain's parts, the allergy sheet and each guest's full answers. The Captain sends the answers to the departments from here.

Open the crew page
The Charter Brief tab writes the department briefs with SABAI's own Claude account, so it will not open for you. You can press Send to the departments: in test mode the emails only go to mate@my-sabai.com.

Privacy

Who sees the answers
SABAI's crew and the yacht's purser, only to prepare for the charter.
How long they are kept
Deleted 30 days after the charter ends, unless a guest ticks Keep to keep their preferences for a future charter.
Guest photos
Optional. Shown only on the crew page, beside each guest's name. Photos do not go in emails. They are deleted with the answers.
Not asked for
Passport details.
Emails to guests
None from the system. The main contact shares guest links themselves.
